WoW Stats Guide

Primary Stats

Strength: Increases attack power for Warriors, Paladins, Death Knights
Agility: Increases attack power and critical strike for Hunters, Rogues, Druids, etc.
Intellect: Increases spell power and mana for Mages, Warlocks, Priests, etc.

Secondary Stats

Critical Strike: Chance to deal double damage or healing
Haste: Increases attack and casting speed
Mastery: Enhances your specialization's unique effect
Versatility: Increases damage/healing done and reduces damage taken
Leech: Returns a portion of damage/healing as health over time
Avoidance: Reduces area-of-effect damage taken
Speed: Increases movement speed
