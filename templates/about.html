About WoW Character Dashboard

What is this?

This dashboard helps World of Warcraft players quickly look up character information including:

  • Character level and item level
  • Primary and secondary stats
  • Mythic+ weekly progress
  • Best dungeon runs

How to use:

Enter your character name and realm on the home page to get instant stats and progress information.

Data Source:

All data comes from the official Blizzard WoW API and updates in real-time.
